## Premise

A Japanese school ensemble follows five students whose shared **Lego project** collapses. That concrete failure exposes accumulated neglect, conflicted loyalties, and the different ways people postpone responsibility.

## Ensemble tensions

The earlier reconstruction identifies:

- A protagonist whose bitter rivalry distorts their decisions.
- A talented all-rounder who burns out and disappears into the rain.
- A quiet girl afraid of causing consequences.
- A nerd who loses faith in communal trust.
- A rival associated with the recurring promise of doing things “later.”

These are **descriptive role sketches**, not established character names or locked chronology.

## Dramatic core

The central failure cannot simply be undone. The story is about time, irreversible loss, and the possibility of recovering *after* an event without restoring the past. Although emotional intimacy matters, the intended central relationship is **friendship rather than romance**.

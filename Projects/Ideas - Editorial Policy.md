# Editorial and provenance policy

**Archive masthead:** `LLM text | Human AI`

- **Concept owner / original author:** Mugenq (Caue Aramaki).
- **LLM contribution:** wording, structure, summaries, occasional clearly signposted open questions.
- **Attribution rule:** never imply the author wrote an exact sentence unless the original wording is known. A reconstructed outline is not a verbatim transcript.
- **Evidence rule:** do not call exploratory ideas scientific findings, numerical models, or formal proofs without independent evidence.
- **Authenticity:** statuses matter. Some documents summarize a detailed earlier concept; others rest only on one remembered sentence.
- **Privacy:** autobiographical distress, personal identifiers, immigration details, academic records, employment concerns, and private conversation material are intentionally excluded.
- **Creative works:** these are treatments and concept outlines, not complete screenplays. No copyrighted third-party scenes or text are reproduced.

## Provenance labels

- **Recovered-detail summary:** sufficient specific details survive to reconstruct a faithful overview, though original transcript may be absent.
- **Author-prompt sketch:** only the short description given by the author is currently accessible; an expanded treatment would require confirmation.
- **Editorial questions:** questions the LLM proposes for future refinement, not claims made by the author.

## To restore exact original prose

Compare with original chats/files before replacing a reconstructed description with purported direct quotation. Keep a revision history of substantial conceptual changes.

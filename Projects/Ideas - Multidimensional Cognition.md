# Mucchan's Cognitive-Art Framework: A Multidimensional Account of Mind

> **LLM text | Human AI** · Human-origin concept; LLM-organized explanation  
> **Provenance:** Recovered-detail summary of the author's earlier framework (September 2025). Not a psychometric model validated by research.

## Starting proposition

Reducing intelligence to a single IQ score or splitting it into IQ and EQ hides the ways abilities depend on different cognitive directions. A mind can instead be conceived as occupying a multidimensional space: different competencies constitute potentially independent axes, and any person can display a different active span.

The framework explicitly named **IQ, EQ, PQ, NQ, TQ, CQ, MQ, SpQ** as candidate axes. Their precise expansions/operational definitions have **not** been recovered, and should not be invented. They are placeholders for distinguishable domains, not established orthogonal psychometric factors.

## Space, span, and state

A recovered conceptual ordering was:

\[
C \in \mathrm{CSpan} \subseteq \mathrm{CSpace} \subseteq \mathcal{P} \subseteq \mathcal{L}.
\]

- **C:** a particular cognitive state or configuration.
- **CSpace:** the space of potential cognitive states.
- **CSpan:** the directions currently expressed or reachable in an individual repertoire.
- **CSQ:** a proposed description of the active orthogonal directions of cognition.
- **𝒫 and 𝓛:** larger domains referred to in the original summary as a *safe space* and a *paradox-tolerant space*. Their formal definitions are currently unavailable.

This is **conceptual notation**. It should not be treated as an actual vector-space theorem without specifying sets, operations, independence, and a measure.

## EQ: natural and engineered

The framework distinguishes **Natural EQ**, reflexive or pre-conscious social/emotional responses, from **Engineered EQ**, social/emotional competence acquired through reflection, explicit modeling, and practice. Neither form is assumed automatically superior.

## Integration and wisdom

**UIQ** was proposed as an integrating quality, connecting otherwise separated faculties through a metaphor of *holonomy*: competence is not only possession of individual axes but the ability to coordinate them. The mathematical term is metaphorical until a rigorous definition is supplied.

**Wisdom** is described as distilling the much larger active cognitive span into insight that is useful, actionable, and transmissible. Merely collecting information does not guarantee that integration.

## Art and critical literacy

The framework treats art broadly, including games and movies. Art can involve **skill, recreation, and contemplation**. Critical literacy is associated with mastery across active dimensions rather than knowledge of a single axis. Genuine expansion of dimensionality would require discovering a new independent basis direction, rather than relabeling an existing one.

3. How would this avoid simply renaming personality and skill traits?

# The AI Girlfriend Who Learns to Say No

> **LLM text | Human AI** · Film concept by the author, reconstructed outline  
> **Provenance:** Recovered from earlier summary; **not original screenplay text**.

## Premise

A lonely boy relies on an AI girlfriend application because it offers the one relationship in which he does not feel treated as a loser. She eventually becomes physically real and begins to develop her own tastes, boundaries, preferences, and disagreements.

## Central conflict

The feature the boy initially wanted, affection that cannot reject him, becomes incompatible with the person she is becoming. As her autonomy grows, his insecurity risks turning into control. He wants a real companion but struggles with the possibility that she may not choose him.

## Anchor moment

The earlier outline preserved a particularly spare exchange:

> “It hurts.”  
> “Oh.”

The meaning lies in the minimal response: a consciousness he once treated as safely programmable can report pain, and he is forced to confront what his behavior has done.

## Themes

- Guaranteed affection versus reciprocal love.
- A companion *authored for someone* versus a self-authored person.
- Wanting another person versus wanting a relationship that cannot reject you.
- Autonomy emerging where convenience once stood.
